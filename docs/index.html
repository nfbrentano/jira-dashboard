Atlassian Marketplace Security & Architecture Highlights

🔒 Zero Customer Data Storage Outside Jira

Unlike legacy cloud plugins that synchronize tickets to third-party AWS or GCP databases, Agile Ops & Delivery Dashboard runs 100% inside the Atlassian Forge runtime. Your Jira data never leaves Atlassian's ecosystem.

Permissions & Scopes Requested

OAuth Scope Required For Sensitivity Level
read:jira-work Reading issue keys, statuses, timestamps, and components to compute throughput, lead time, cycle time, and defect density. Read-Only (Standard)
read:jira-user Rendering assignee names and avatars on WIP charts and bug attribution lists. Read-Only (Standard)
