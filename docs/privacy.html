1. Overview & Architecture

This Privacy Policy describes how Agile Ops & Delivery Dashboard for Jira ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when installed by organizations or individual users ("you", "Customer") within Atlassian Jira Cloud instances.

🛡️ 100% Serverless & Forge Native Guarantee

The App is engineered exclusively using Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's trusted serverless app development platform. The App runs strictly within Atlassian Cloud infrastructure and your local browser session. We do not operate any external application servers, external databases, or third-party tracking relays.

All delivery velocity, throughput, lead/cycle time, WIP limit, and quality calculations are computed ephemerally in-memory inside the user's browser via the official Atlassian Forge bridge (@forge/bridge).

2. Information Accessed & Permissions (Scopes)

To deliver operational agility charts and analytics, the App requests only the minimal necessary OAuth scopes defined in the Atlassian Forge manifest:

Forge Scope Purpose Data Accessed
read:jira-work Generate delivery flow, velocity, and defect metrics. Issue keys, summaries, issue types, status transitions, created/resolved timestamps, resolution status, components, and labels.
read:jira-user Display responsible owners on defect and WIP limit charts. User display names and public Atlassian avatar URLs strictly as rendered in Jira.

The App never requests write permissions, administrative permissions, or access to sensitive issue attachments, passwords, or billing details.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Our core privacy principle is simple: Your data remains in your Jira instance.

  • No External Storage: We do not store, duplicate, or mirror your Jira issues, project metadata, or team information on any remote servers.
  • Ephemeral Computation: Issue data fetched via the Jira REST API is held temporarily in the browser's active memory (RAM) while you view the dashboard. When you navigate away or close your browser tab, this data is automatically purged.
  • User Configuration: UI preferences (such as selected project filters, date windows, or custom WIP thresholds) are saved strictly inside your browser's local storage (localStorage) or Atlassian's secure Forge Storage API.

4. Subprocessors & Third-Party Services

Because the App is built natively on Atlassian Forge:

  • No External Subprocessors: We do not employ third-party cloud hosting providers, external analytics vendors (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel), or advertising trackers.
  • Atlassian Cloud Platform: All hosting, code execution, and data transmission are handled solely by Atlassian Pty Ltd in accordance with their Atlassian Privacy Policy and Atlassian Trust Center.

5. Security & Isolation Controls

The App adheres to the security criteria established by the Atlassian Marketplace Security Program:

  • Authentication & Authorization: Authentication is handled natively by Atlassian Cloud. Only authenticated users with legitimate permissions in your Jira tenant can access the dashboard.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP): Forge enforces rigorous Content Security Policies that block unauthorized outbound network connections, cross-site scripting (XSS), and third-party script injection.
  • Role-Based Access Control: The App respects Jira's native project permissions. A user will only see issues and projects they are already authorized to view in Jira.
  • Encrypted in Transit: All communications between the user interface and Atlassian APIs utilize TLS 1.3 encryption.

6. GDPR, LGPD & International User Rights

We comply with global data protection standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Brazilian Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Erasure & Access: Since we do not harvest or retain personal data on external servers, uninstalling the App immediately severs all access. Any local settings stored in your browser can be cleared by purging browser storage or Atlassian Forge app data.
  • Data Portability & Minimization: Data accessed is strictly limited to the minimum necessary for the dashboard's operation.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or Atlassian Forge platform requirements. When updates occur, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.
