2. Description of Service

The App provides interactive delivery analytics, agile flow metrics, and operational diagnostics for Jira Cloud, including:

  • Throughput velocity trends (weekly and monthly issue completion rates).
  • Lead Time and Cycle Time distribution analytics across status workflows.
  • Work-In-Progress (WIP) limit monitoring and bottleneck detection alerts.
  • Backlog aging categorizations and stale issue diagnostics.
  • Defect density and quality analytics by assignee, priority, and component.

The App operates as a native Atlassian Forge application and does not transmit customer Jira data to third-party servers.
